Our Devotional Life

Daily Prayer

We pray the Holy Rosary every day. It's our main spiritual weapon and the foundation of our spiritual lives. We pray the daily Holy Face prayers, "Lord show us thy face and we shall be saved." Followed by an Our Father, Haily Mary, and Glory be...

Holy Rosary confraternity

Relic History & Study

We are members of the League of St. Matrin. We explore the historical lineage of St. Veronica's Veil, the saints of the Holy Face, and its significance to our times.

Fellowship
