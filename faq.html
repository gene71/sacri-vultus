FAQ | Holy Face Devotion Meetup

What is the focus of our meetup group?

Our group is dedicated to devotional contemplation, prayers of reparation, and historical study surrounding the Holy Face of Christ and Vale of Veronica.

"Vera Effigies Sacri Vultus Domini Nostri Jesu Christi..."

Meetings include prayers of reparation from the Holy Face manual, recitation of the Chaplet of the Holy Face, and discussion on sacred art and tradition.

When was the group started?

It was started approximately 3 years ago (2023) after attending a mission given by Fr. Carney on this devotion.

How was the group started?

It was started after speaking with Fr. Carney about starting a chapter.

Why was the group started?

It was started to provide an opportunity for people living south of the DFW area to have an alternative to monthly meetings in the DFW area.

Where are the monthly meetings held?

They are held in the town of Cleburne Texas, about 1 hour south of the DFW area.

Who is this group associated with?

It operates as a chapter under the League of St. Martin.

We were formed in 2023. see map -> League of St. Marting

Who can attend this meetup?

This meetup is intended for Roman Catholics intrested in participating in the Holy Face devotion.

To participate in this devoution you will need to enroll in the Archconfraternity.
